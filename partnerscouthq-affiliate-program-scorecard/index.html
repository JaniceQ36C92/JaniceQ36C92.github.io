How scoring works

Weighted score = (audience × 30% + product × 20% + economics × 15% + attribution × 10% + reliability × 15% + clarity × 10%) ÷ 5 × 100. Each rating is 1 (weak) to 5 (strong). Scores reflect your judgment, not an independent rating.
