# Affiliate Program Evaluation Scorecard

A free, local-first worksheet for comparing manually researched affiliate offers.

## Weighted scoring

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Audience fit | 30% |
| Product quality | 20% |
| Commission economics | 15% |
| Cookie / attribution | 10% |
| Payout reliability | 15% |
| Terms clarity | 10% |

Each dimension is rated 1–5. Weighted score = sum(rating × weight) ÷ 5 × 100. The score is a personal research aid, not an objective program rating or earnings forecast.

## Verify before making decisions

Confirm current commission rates, recurring-payment rules, attribution windows, eligible countries, prohibited traffic sources, reversal rules, payout thresholds, hold periods, tax requirements, and disclosure obligations with the official advertiser or network. Terms can change without notice.

Records are stored in browser local storage, not uploaded or synchronized. Local storage is not encrypted. Do not enter passwords, private account data, or confidential commercial information. Export a backup before clearing browser data.
