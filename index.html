Designed to keep your working data local

The calculators process inputs in the page. The job tracker and both scorecards use your browser's local storage. There is no account, database, analytics beacon, advertising tag, or upload endpoint in these tools. Export a backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device.

  • Do not store passwords or identity documents.
  • Verify job details with the original employer.
  • Verify affiliate terms with the advertiser or network.
  • Verify software prices and security claims with vendors.
  • Use financial outputs as estimates, not professional advice.
