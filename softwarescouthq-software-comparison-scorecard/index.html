How to use the scores

Rate every option against the same business requirements, using evidence—not brand recognition.

  • Feature fit 30%: workflows and critical requirements
  • Security 25%: data handling, controls and assurances
  • Usability 15%: onboarding and accessibility
  • Integrations 15%: APIs and current stack
  • Support 15%: channels and commitments

Formula: sum(rating × weight) ÷ 5 × 100.

Not assessed means Unrated, not 0. Ratings are your judgments; none represent a verified vendor audit.

Understand total cost

Est. TCO = (per-seat monthly rate × seats + other monthly costs) × comparison months + one-time costs.

The same horizon is applied to every product. Real costs may include taxes, consumption, contract minimums, migrations, and discounts not captured here. All costs must be entered in USD.
