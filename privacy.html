Privacy and local data handling

Last updated: November 11, 2026

These tools are static browser applications. They do not include a server-side account, database, analytics script, advertising tag, or form-submission endpoint.

What stays in your browser

  • The calculator processes values in the current page and does not save or upload them.
  • The job tracker stores job records in local storage in the browser profile you are using.
  • The affiliate scorecard stores your manually entered program records in local storage in the browser profile you are using.
  • The software comparison scorecard stores your manually entered vendor names, ratings, cost estimates, and notes in local storage in the browser profile you are using.

Your responsibilities

  • Local storage is not encrypted storage, a cloud backup, or cross-device synchronization.
  • Anyone who can use the same browser profile may be able to see locally saved records.
  • Export a backup before clearing site data, changing browsers, or changing devices.
  • Do not enter passwords, identity documents, bank details, confidential employer information, or other sensitive personal data.

External links

Tool pages may link to related websites and official resources. When you follow an external link, the destination's own privacy policy and terms apply. No external page is embedded inside these tools.
